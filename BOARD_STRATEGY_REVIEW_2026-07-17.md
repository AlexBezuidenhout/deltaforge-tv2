### The cross-venue opportunity, precisely

The worthwhile cross-venue idea is **not** “buy opposite positions whenever two
titles look similar.” It is:

1. Prove a terminal relation such as `A implies B` from both rulebooks.
2. Wait until observable event state removes deadline or cancellation states
   that could break the relation.
3. For `A implies B`, buy `NO(A) + YES(B)`, which pays at least $1 in every
   permitted state.
4. Trade only when full-depth asks, both venue fees, adverse ticks and orphan
   unwind stress cost less than that minimum payout.

The Trump speech pair satisfied this logic after the speech began on schedule.
At the best stored observation, the stressed modeled profit was $55.59 on
$253.40 deployed, or 21.94%. Public prints existed on both legs and both
contracts later resolved NO. This is a credible missed trade and the best lead
in the entire audit. It is still only one retrospectively selected event and
cross-venue submission is non-atomic. The next objective is not tuning its
threshold; it is discovering many more rules-proved implication/exclusion
relations and measuring their forward frequency, capacity and orphan rate.

The 27 manually reviewed pairs contained **zero exact identities**. Therefore:

- the exact-title/parity cross-venue bot is **not worth trading**;
- generic convergence between merely similar contracts is **not risk-neutral**;
- the state-conditioned relation engine **is worth building and testing**;
- Kalshi execution from Dublin remains blocked unless venue eligibility is
  confirmed in writing.

## Evidence standard

The decision metric is not dashboard balance, win rate or the number of quote
rows. A candidate needs:

- a frozen mechanism and code version before the evaluation cohort;
- at least 300 fresh independent markets and 14 calendar days;
- executable ask/bid depth, partial and non-fills, fees and a 2× cost stress;
- positive P&L in both chronological halves;
- a market-clustered lower confidence bound above zero;
- family-wise correction across every inspected strategy and variant;
- no single asset, event or day dominating the result;
- orphan-leg and immediate-unwind accounting for multi-leg trades.

## Why systematically losing bots cannot simply be reversed

The opposite token's executable ask is not `1 - original fill`. Usually:

`YES ask + NO ask = 1 + spread + inventory premium`.

Flipping direction pays the other spread, slippage and fees, faces different
depth, and may select a different set of fills. If the original signal is
uninformative and costs cause the loss, both sides lose. The executable inverse
autopsy preserved latency, opposite asks, displayed depth, partial/non-fills,
2× fees, chronological halves and multiple-testing correction. It found zero
inverted strategy eligible for promotion. No signal was flipped.

## Ranked opportunities to pursue

These are research priorities, not projected profits.

1. **State-conditioned payoff relations.** Encode implication, mutual
   exclusion, exhaustiveness and dynamic event state across venues. The one
   speech event is a real lead, and the deterministic proof engine is now in
   production paper mode. Main risks are semantic rule tails, venue eligibility
   and orphan legs.
2. **Same-venue combinatorial sports identities.** Build a condition graph over
   moneyline, spread and total markets where payoff algebra can create locks.
   A recent NBA study reports far more combinatorial than single-market
   opportunities, but short durations and low displayed capacity; it is useful
   external motivation, not DeltaForge P&L.
3. **Repaired ordered strikes and disjoint ranges.** The proof machinery now
   handles price predicates correctly. Expand the daily panel only under a
   frozen universe and retain non-atomic execution stress.
4. **Reward-aware complete-set making.** Continue only as a queue/adverse-
   selection experiment. Promotion requires real merge rates, authenticated
   reward attribution and positive orphan-inclusive P&L.
5. **Sports reference pricing.** Compare executable Polymarket prices with
   de-vigged sportsbook/Betfair distributions, then trade only residual edge at
   the ask. This needs lawful, timestamped reference feeds.
6. **Options-derived digital probabilities.** Use liquid Deribit option surfaces
   for daily BTC/ETH threshold and range fair values. Five-minute direction is
   too close to a noisy coin flip for slow model alpha; longer strikes are a
   better fit for option-implied distributions.
7. **CEX/DEX basis and funding outside Polymarket.** The VPS and multi-network
   capture are structurally more suited to deterministic funding/basis and
   settlement relationships than trying to predict every five-minute binary.
8. **AI-assisted semantic discovery with deterministic certification.** A model
   may propose related contracts and event-state updates; only the finite-state
   compiler plus a human rule review may approve economics.

Latency should remain an experimental variable, not a sales claim. Build for
20–50 ms internal reaction, then require incremental post-fee P&L across fixed
latency profiles before optimizing further. H47's -$499.60 directly shows that
seeing one transport path earlier is not automatically tradable edge.
